# G6PD-ARON — Risk Classification

## Purpose

This classification system is used to describe the potential risk of medications in patients with G6PD deficiency.

The classification is intended for educational and clinical reference purposes.

---

## 🔴 High Risk — Avoid

Medications for which there is substantial evidence of clinically important hemolytic risk in G6PD deficiency.

### Recommendation

Avoid when possible.

If use is considered essential, specialist assessment and appropriate monitoring may be required.

---

## 🟠 Medium Risk — Use With Caution

Medications for which there is evidence suggesting a potential risk of hemolysis, but the risk may depend on clinical circumstances, dose, patient characteristics, or available alternatives.

### Recommendation

Use only when appropriate, considering alternatives and monitoring when indicated.

---

## 🟢 Low-to-No Risk

Medications for which available evidence does not indicate a clinically important increased risk of hemolysis at standard therapeutic use.

### Recommendation

Standard precautions.

---

## ⚪ Insufficient Evidence / No Recommendation

There is insufficient evidence to confidently classify the medication as low, medium, or high risk.

### Recommendation

Do not interpret this category as "safe."

Clinical judgment and additional evidence should be considered.

---

## 🟣 Conflicting Evidence

Reliable sources provide different conclusions regarding the risk of the medication in G6PD deficiency.

### Recommendation

The disagreement between sources should be displayed clearly.

The user should be directed to the underlying references rather than receiving an oversimplified "safe" or "unsafe" label.

---

## Important Factors

Risk assessment may depend on:

- Dose
- Route of administration
- Duration of treatment
- Severity of G6PD deficiency
- Patient age
- Acute infection
- Concurrent medications
- Other clinical conditions
- Available evidence

---

## Source Classification vs Verified Classification

G6PD-ARON maintains two separate concepts:

### Original Classification

The classification reported in the original source document supplied for the project.

### Verified Classification

The classification assigned after reviewing current clinical guidelines, regulatory information, pharmacogenomic recommendations, and published evidence.

These classifications must not be automatically assumed to be identical.

---

## Evidence Level

Each medication should include an evidence level where sufficient information is available.

Suggested levels:

- High
- Moderate
- Low
- Very Low
- Conflicting
- Insufficient

---

## Disclaimer

G6PD-ARON is an educational and reference project.

It is not intended to replace professional medical judgment, official prescribing information, or specialist consultation.
