## Important Note

This file represents the classification found in the original source document.

It does NOT represent the final evidence-based classification of the G6PD-ARON project.

The project will separately verify each drug against current clinical guidelines, pharmacogenomic recommendations, regulatory information, and published evidence.

A drug may therefore have:

- Original classification: High Risk
- Verified classification: Low-to-no Risk

or another classification after evidence review.

The original classification must remain preserved for transparency.
